# Credits

Spider-Punisher (`spider-punisher-tw`) is a fan-made pack by **T-Wrecks**.

- Card design and art direction: T-Wrecks. Cover: his own box art.
- Card template: Hitch. The cards are rebuilt on the Hitch templates from his art (English and Spanish) by Francisco
  Merchán, from his files of 2026-11-19, which take the curators' wording notes.
- Spanish translation and layout: Francisco Merchán.
- Source: BluefireF5ran's curated repository https://github.com/bluefireF5ran/mc-digital-custom-packs (commit
  `c131319`, folder `spider_punisher_tw`).
- Ability scripts and the conversion to the pack format: BluefireF5ran. The starter deck
  (`decks/spider-punisher-tw-leadership.json`) is the curators' Leadership deck.

The card data was converted from MarvelCDB-shaped records with the mod SDK's `importMarvelCdbPack`.
Character art is Marvel comics art, as used by the author.

Marvel Champions: The Card Game is the property of Fantasy Flight Games and Marvel.

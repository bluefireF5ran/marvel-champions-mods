# Credits

"Don't Make Me Angry!" (`hulk-angry-bf`) is an optional house rule for the official Hulk by **Abis Mal** and
**Bluefire**.

- The card: "Permanent. Setup." It starts in play; Hulk's identity-specific cards discarded by an ability come back
  tucked under it, to be played or spent as if they were in hand (twice per phase).
- Card face, English and Spanish: Bluefire, on the Hitch card template. Spanish text: Bluefire.
- Starter deck "Hulk's Lab Partners - Protection": the curators.
- Source: BluefireF5ran's curated repository https://github.com/bluefireF5ran/mc-digital-custom-packs at commit
  `c131319ef5bafe0687c64a1b0bb901e394975122` (folder `hulk_angry_bf`).
- The conversion to the pack format: this project.

Marvel Champions: The Card Game, its cards and their art are the property of Fantasy Flight Games and Marvel.

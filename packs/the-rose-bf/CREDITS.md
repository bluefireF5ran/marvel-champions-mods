# Credits

The Rose (`the-rose-bf`) is a fan-made villain scenario by **BigDarsh** and **Bluefire**.

- Card design: BigDarsh and Bluefire (May 2025; simplified version of 2026-11-18).
- Card template: Hitch. English and Spanish cards by Francisco Merchán.
- Source: BluefireF5ran's curated repository https://github.com/bluefireF5ran/mc-digital-custom-packs (commit
  `c131319`, folder `the_rose_bf`).
- Ability scripts and the conversion to the pack format: BluefireF5ran.

The card data was converted from MarvelCDB-shaped records with the mod SDK's `importMarvelCdbPack`.
Character art is Marvel comics art.
